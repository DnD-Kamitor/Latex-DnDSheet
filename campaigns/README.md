# The Shadow of Mars

**A 5-Session D&D 5e Campaign in a Roman Fantasy World**

*Version 1.0 - January 2026*

## Overview

This complete campaign module follows Guy Sclanders' game master methodologies for creating epic, memorable campaigns. Set in an alternate Roman world where magic and gods are real, players must navigate duty, ambition, and divine wrath.

## Campaign Details

**Duration**: 5 sessions (approximately 15-20 hours of gameplay)

**Player Levels**: 3 → 7 (one level per session)

**Players**: 3-5 recommended

**Tone**: Gritty fantasy with moral complexity

**Themes**: Duty vs. Free Will, fate vs. ambition, divine intervention

## Campaign Structure

### Chapter 1: Blood and Omens
Party discovers a cursed artifact at a Roman frontier fort.

### Chapter 2: The Tribune's Gambit
Political intrigue and betrayal during a Germanic raid.

### Chapter 3: Through the Dark Forest
Journey to destroy the artifact while facing corruption.

### Chapter 4: Return to Rome
Uncover a senatorial conspiracy in the heart of the Empire.

### Chapter 5: The Wrath of Mars
Face divine judgment from the god of war himself.

## Guy Sclanders' Principles Applied

This campaign demonstrates professional TTRPG design using Sclanders' proven methodologies:

### Master Plot
Clear story progression: Discovery → Corruption → Destruction → Divine Judgment

### Strong Theme
Every session explores **Duty vs. Free Will** - players choose between Rome, the gods, and their conscience.

### Villain Archetypes
- **Legate Corvinus**: Blunt Force Nemesis (direct opposition)
- **Senator Brutus**: Never Present (manipulating from shadows)
- **Mars**: Divine Antagonist (ultimate challenge)

### Player Agency
Multiple valid paths through each session. No "correct" answers, only consequences.

### Meaningful Sacrifice
Victories require genuine sacrifice - items, abilities, or NPCs.

### Pressure Cooker Design
Fast-paced scenes with incomplete information force quick decisions.

## Running This Campaign

### Preparation
1. Read the entire campaign once before Session 0
2. Run Session 0 with players to establish expectations
3. Print or bookmark NPC stat blocks (Appendix)
4. Prepare battle maps for key encounters

### Session Pacing
Each session is designed for 3-4 hours of play:
- 30 min: Recap and opening scene
- 2-2.5 hours: Main encounters and roleplay
- 30 min: Climax and conclusion

### Adapting to Your Group
- **Combat-heavy groups**: Add more encounters to scenes 2-3
- **Roleplay-heavy groups**: Expand NPC conversations and moral dilemmas
- **Fast-paced groups**: Combine scenes or reduce investigation time
